Case study · Healthcare

A private hospital booked 85 appointments from 205 ad leads in one quarter.

Previously, staff replied to each ad lead manually and booked 10 to 20% of them. We set up the AI agent to reply to every lead, follow up, contact older leads again and book appointments in the hospital calendar.

Leads from ads205
Booked before (manual replies)10 to 20%
Booked with AI agent85 · 41%

Private hospital, One quarter (Q4). Source: FlySquid CRM pipeline report. 85 of 205 is 41.5%. Client name shared on request.

THE SITUATION

Leads were handled manually

  • Staff replied to each ad lead manually
  • Follow-ups were inconsistent
  • Many leads received no follow-up
  • Between 10 and 20% of leads booked
WHAT WE DID

We set up the AI agent for all leads

  • Replies within seconds, 24 hours a day
  • Qualification through normal conversation
  • Questions and objections handled
  • Scheduled follow-up when a lead did not reply
  • Old leads from earlier weeks contacted again
  • Appointments booked into the hospital calendar
WHAT CHANGED

The booking rate more than doubled

  • 205 leads in the quarter
  • 85 appointments booked
  • About 41% of leads booked
  • No manual follow-up by hospital staff
