Which services does FlySquid offer?

Seven: AI Sales Agent, Paid Advertising, Creative Content and Social Media, Websites and Landing Pages, SEO and Search Marketing, Email Marketing, and Influencer Marketing.

Can I hire FlySquid for one service only?

Yes. The services are available separately or together. Most clients start with the AI agent or paid ads and add other services later.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Meta, Google, TikTok and Snap.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. You pay the ad platforms directly, as well as influencer fees and gifting costs.

Do you run photo or video shoots?

No. We produce ad creative and social content from the brand assets, photos and video you already have.
