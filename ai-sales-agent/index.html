How it works

How the agent handles a lead, in six steps.

The agent handles replies and follow-up. Your team receives booked appointments in the calendar.

  1. A lead comes in

    From an ad, your website, an Instagram post or a phone call.

  2. The agent replies within seconds

    It replies in the language the lead used: Arabic, English or French.

  3. It answers questions and qualifies

    It asks about the service, budget and timing. It answers from your price lists and FAQs, within the rules you set.

  4. It books the appointment

    The appointment is added to your calendar. The lead receives a confirmation and a reminder.

  5. If there is no reply, it follows up

    It sends follow-up messages on a set schedule.

  6. It contacts older leads again

    Leads who enquired in earlier weeks and did not book receive a new message.

Custom setup

What each agent setup includes.

Each agent is built for one client. Our team writes it, tests it and updates it based on your feedback.

Instructions

Written instructions for how the agent communicates for your brand, including tone and level of detail, in English and Arabic.

Knowledge base

Built from your website, price lists, onboarding answers and voice notes. The agent answers only from this information.

Your rules

Rules for pricing, handover to staff, opening hours and topics the agent must not discuss.

Human handover

Complaints, medical questions and requests to speak to staff are passed to your team with the full conversation.

CRM and calendar

All conversations, pipeline stages and bookings are recorded in FlySquid CRM.

Feedback and updates

Questions

Frequently asked questions about the AI agent.

See all questions →
Which channels does it cover?

WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, SMS, web chat and voice calls. Most clients in MENA and the GCC start with WhatsApp and Instagram, because most of their leads use those channels.

Will leads know they are talking to an AI?

You decide. We can set the agent up to say it is an AI assistant, and we recommend doing so.

Is the AI agent a chatbot with fixed buttons?

No. It writes its own replies in normal conversation. We write its instructions and build its knowledge base from your price lists, website and FAQs. It follows the rules you set, for example not quoting prices in chat, or passing medical questions to a staff member.

What happens when the agent cannot answer?

It hands the conversation to your team. You decide the triggers: a complaint, a medical question, a request to speak to a person, or a question outside its knowledge base. Your team sees the full conversation in the CRM.

Does it really work in Arabic?

Yes. Our team writes and reviews the agent’s Arabic and English text, including the level of formality. It replies in the language the lead writes in.

How fast does the AI agent reply?

Within seconds, 24 hours a day, on the channel the lead used: WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, SMS, web chat or phone.

What information does the agent use to answer?

Only its knowledge base, built from your website, price lists, onboarding answers and voice notes, and always within the rules you set.

Where do booked appointments go?

Into your calendar. Every conversation, pipeline stage and booking is recorded in FlySquid CRM, and the lead receives a confirmation and a reminder.

What does the agent do if a lead stops replying?

It sends follow-up messages on a set schedule. Leads who enquired in earlier weeks and did not book receive a new message later.

How are changes made to the agent?

You send us feedback by text or voice note. We update the agent the same working day and keep a log of changes.
