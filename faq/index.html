FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Short answers about what we do, how the AI sales agent works, and how working with us is set up.

About FlySquid

What does FlySquid do?

FlySquid is a growth marketing agency. We manage paid ads on Meta, Google, TikTok and Snap, and set up an AI sales agent that replies to leads on WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and SMS within seconds and books appointments.

Where is FlySquid based, and which regions do you work in?

Fly Squid FZ LLC is registered in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. Our team works remotely with clients in North America, MENA and the GCC.

Which languages do you work in?

Arabic, English and French. The AI agent replies in the language the lead writes in.

Which industries do you work with?

Clinics and hospitals, home care agencies, real estate, schools and education, and e-commerce. We also work with other service businesses.

The AI sales agent

Is the AI agent a chatbot with fixed buttons?

No. It writes its own replies in normal conversation. We write its instructions and build its knowledge base from your price lists, website and FAQs. It follows the rules you set, for example not quoting prices in chat, or passing medical questions to a staff member.

What happens when the agent cannot answer?

It hands the conversation to your team. You decide the triggers: a complaint, a medical question, a request to speak to a person, or a question outside its knowledge base. Your team sees the full conversation in the CRM.

Does it really work in Arabic?

Yes. Our team writes and reviews the agent’s Arabic and English text, including the level of formality. It replies in the language the lead writes in.

Which channels does the AI agent work on?

WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, SMS, web chat and voice. It replies on the channel the lead used.

We already have an agency for ads. Can you only do the AI agent?

Yes. The agent works with leads from any source, including campaigns run by another agency.

Working with us

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Our standard terms are month to month with 30 days’ written notice. You pay ad platforms directly for your ad spend.

How long until we are live?

Onboarding takes about two weeks for most clients. It depends on how quickly we receive your price lists, FAQs and account access.

How is billing handled?

One invoice a month, billed in advance in US dollars and due within 7 days. You pay Meta, Google and other ad platforms directly for your ad spend.

What is the free competitor audit?
