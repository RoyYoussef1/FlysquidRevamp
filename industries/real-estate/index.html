Problems

Common problems in this sector.

01

Many enquiries are not qualified

Sales agents spend a large part of their time working out which enquiries are from genuine buyers.

02

Overseas buyers are in other time zones

Buyers living abroad often send enquiries outside your office hours.

03

Leads arrive on several platforms

Leads come from Meta forms, TikTok, property portals and Instagram DMs, and are not stored in one place.

Our approach

What we do for real estate.

01

Campaigns by project and buyer

Meta and TikTok campaigns for buyers and sellers, including diaspora audiences abroad.

02

Lead qualification

The agent asks whether the lead wants to buy or rent, and about budget, purpose and timing. It then books a call with a sales agent.

03

One CRM pipeline

Leads from all platforms are recorded in FlySquid CRM with their stage and conversation history.

Rules the agent follows

The agent follows the rules you set.

  • Qualifies budget and purpose before booking
  • Sends matching listings in the chat
  • Books calls in the buyer’s time zone
  • Hands over when a buyer asks for a specific agent
