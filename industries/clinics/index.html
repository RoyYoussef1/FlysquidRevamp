Problems

Common problems in this sector.

01

Front desk staff are busy

The staff who reply on WhatsApp also check patients in, so replies are delayed.

02

Many enquiries arrive after hours

Patients often send enquiries in the evening, when the clinic is closed, and contact other clinics as well.

03

Price questions are handled inconsistently

Price questions go unanswered, or staff quote a price before the doctor has assessed the patient.

Our approach

What we do for clinics and hospitals.

01

Patient advertising

Meta and Google campaigns by treatment and location, with creative that passes platform health rules.

02

An AI agent that follows your clinical rules

It never gives medical advice. It follows your pricing policy. It hands over to a person when it should.

03

Automatic booking and reminders

Bookings, confirmations and reminders are handled automatically. Older enquiries are contacted again.

Rules the agent follows

The agent follows the rules you set.

  • Never quotes prices in chat, if that is your policy
  • Never gives medical advice
  • Collects name, service and preferred time before offering a slot
  • Knows your opening hours and doctors’ schedules
  • Hands over to staff on complaints or clinical questions
Case study · Healthcare

A private hospital booked 85 appointments from 205 ad leads in one quarter.

Previously, staff replied to each ad lead manually and booked 10 to 20% of them. We set up the AI agent to reply to every lead, follow up, contact older leads again and book appointments in the hospital calendar.
