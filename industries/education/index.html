Problems

Common problems in this sector.

01

Admissions teams are small

A small team cannot reply to every enquiry quickly during peak season while also running tours.

02

Most questions are repeated

Parents ask the same questions about places, curriculum, transport and documents. Answering each one manually takes time.

03

Families compare several schools

Parents usually contact more than one school, so a fast reply matters.

Our approach

What we do for schools and education.

01

Campaigns around admissions dates

Paid and social campaigns timed to your intake calendar, by grade and area.

02

Fast answers to parent questions

The agent answers from your admissions guide and leaves fees to your team if that is your policy.

03

Tours and calls booked

Parents choose a slot. Your team gets the child’s grade and the family’s situation before the meeting.

Rules the agent follows

The agent follows the rules you set.

  • Leaves fee discussions to admissions, if that is your policy
  • Answers in the parent’s language
  • Sends the document checklist after booking
  • Hands over on special cases
