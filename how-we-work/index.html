How we work

Our process, onboarding and contract terms.

This page explains how we work with clients, what happens in the first two weeks, and our standard contract terms.

The process

Our process has five stages.

  1. Discovery and research

    We learn about your brand, goals and customers. We review your competitors’ ads on Meta and Google, your website and your online listings.

  2. Strategy and setup

    We set up tracking and the required tools before any ad spend, so that decisions are based on data.

  3. Creative development

    We produce visuals and ad copy for your audience. For the AI agent, we write its instructions and build its knowledge base.

  4. Campaign execution

    We launch and manage the campaigns. We monitor performance, adjust budgets and run tests.

  5. Reporting and optimisation

    We send regular reports covering results, cost and planned changes.

The terms

Standard contract terms.

Month to month

There is no minimum term. Either party can end the contract with 30 days’ written notice.

Billed monthly in advance

One invoice a month, in US dollars, due within 7 days.

Ad spend paid directly

You pay Meta, Google and other platforms directly. You also pay influencer fees and gifting costs.

Not included

We do not run photo or video shoots. Your proposal lists everything that is out of scope.

Standard terms. Your proposal states the exact scope and price for your business.
