How we operate

Three principles we work by.

  1. We report on bookings and sales

    We measure our work by booked appointments and sales, and we manage the steps between the ad and the booking.

  2. We write clearly

    Our reports, proposals and calls avoid jargon.

  3. We work month to month

    Our contracts have no minimum term. You can stop with 30 days’ written notice.
